Accessibility Statement

Commitment to accessibility

Taste & See Creamery is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities. We strive to make our website, www.tasteandseecreamery.com, as accessible as possible to provide an inclusive experience for all visitors.

Accessibility features

  • Alternative text for images to assist screen readers.
  • Keyboard navigability for ease of use.
  • High-contrast text for readability.
  • Clear and simple language throughout our website.

Ongoing improvements

We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of our website by following the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and regularly reviewing our content and features.

Third-party content

While we strive to ensure accessibility across all areas of our website, some third-party content or services may not be fully accessible. We encourage feedback on any accessibility concerns related to such content.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter any accessibility barriers or require assistance, please contact us. We value your feedback and will make every effort to improve your experience.
