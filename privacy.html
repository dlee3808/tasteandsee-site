Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Taste & See Creamery! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information when you visit our website www.tasteandseecreamery.com.

Information we collect

  • Personal information: your name, email address, phone number, and other details provided when placing orders, signing up for newsletters, or contacting us.
  • Payment information: collected by third-party payment processors; we do not directly store payment data.
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and interaction data collected via cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • Process and fulfill orders.
  • Provide customer service and respond to inquiries.
  • Improve our website, products, and services.
  • Send promotional offers, newsletters, and updates (if opted in).
  • Maintain website security and compliance.

Information sharing

  • Service providers: third parties assisting with website operations, payments, and other essential services.
  • Legal requirements: authorities or courts when legally required.

Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. You can manage cookies via your browser settings, though some website features may become limited.

External websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies separately.

Your data rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data, and to opt out of receiving promotional communications. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@tasteandseecreamery.com.

Security measures

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security of online transmissions.

Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted here along with an updated effective date.
